Welche Daten die App verarbeitet

Deine Bestenliste (Punktzahlen mit Datum), der Rekord des Tagesturms und deine Einstellungen (Musik, Sounds, Block-Stil) werden ausschließlich lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Sie verlassen das Gerät durch die App nicht. Wenn du die App deinstallierst oder ihre Daten löschst, sind sie weg.

Die App fordert nur die Berechtigung „Vibration“ an, um beim Spielen kurz zu vibrieren.

Das steuerst du in den Android-Einstellungen; wir haben darauf keinen Zugriff.

What data the app handles

Your leaderboard (scores with dates), your daily tower record and your settings (music, sounds, block style) are stored only on your device. The app never sends them anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

The only permission the app requests is “vibrate”, for short haptic feedback during play.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android backup (for example Google One / Google Drive backup), Android may include the app's local game data in your personal device backup together with your other app data. You control this in your Android settings; we have no access to it.

Google Play

When you download or update the app through Google Play, Google processes data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We only receive aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as install counts) and crash reports without personal identifiers, if you allowed Android to share them.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. If you have questions, feel free to contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If a future version processes data (for example through ads or online leaderboards), we will update this policy beforehand and point it out in the app.
